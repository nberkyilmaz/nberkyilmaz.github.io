Departure Brief

A pre-flight briefing for VFR pilots. It decodes METARs, TAFs and NOTAMs, works out which forecast applies at each point of the route when you'll be there, and checks it against your own minimums, citing the report behind every line.

  • 5 live weather feeds through a Cloudflare Worker
  • 899 automated tests in CI
  • TypeScript
  • React
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Fastify
  • PostgreSQL

About

On the ground and in the air

As a Software Developer at Vretta, I help keep our assessment platform fast, secure, and reliable when thousands of students log in to write an exam at the same time. I love the detective work behind it: load testing, digging through logs, and tracing a slowdown back to its root cause. Knowing a student's big day goes smoothly because of that work is what motivates me most.

Away from work, my favourite place is the cockpit. I'm a private pilot flying a Cessna 172, and nothing clears my head like a calm evening in the air. I often fly friends and family to camping spots and beaches that are hard to reach by car. I also play soccer and go on long bike rides around the city to unwind after a busy week.
